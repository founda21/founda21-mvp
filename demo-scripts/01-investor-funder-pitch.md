# Founda21 Demo Script — Investor / Funder Pitch

**Length:** 5:00
**Audience:** Corporate ESD teams, ESD fund managers, DFIs/government, universities, accelerators/incubators, impact investors/VCs
**Tone:** Confident, credible, outcome-focused. This is a sales narrative, not a feature tour — every screen should answer "why does this matter to a funder."
**Music:** Low, professional, builds slightly at the 3:30 "results" beat, resolves by 4:45.
**Voice:** One narrator throughout, third person ("Founda21 does X"), no on-screen presenter needed.

---

## 0:00–0:30 — Cold open: the problem

**Screen:** Black slate with bold white text, no app yet.

**On-screen text (sequential, ~2s each):**
- "Every funder says they back the best founders."
- "Almost none of them can prove it."
- "Pitch decks lie. Self-assessments lie. Gut feel doesn't scale."

**Voiceover:**
"Every accelerator, DFI, and corporate ESD programme claims to back the best founders. But readiness is usually judged by a pitch deck and a gut feeling — not evidence. Founda21 replaces that gut feeling with a standard."

---

## 0:30–1:00 — Introduce the standard

**Screen:** Cut to the Founda21 landing page (`/`). Slow zoom into the headline.

**Voiceover:**
"Founda21 is a 21-checkpoint founder readiness standard, scored by AI across five dimensions, in three stages — Idea & Reality, Company & Traction, and Investor & Deal Readiness. A founder doesn't pass by talking. They pass by producing real artifacts that get scored against a fixed rubric — the same rubric, every time."

**Visual beat:** Highlight the tagline "Know which founders are actually investable" and the "21 checkpoints, five dimensions, scored by AI" subhead with a subtle box/glow.

---

## 1:00–1:45 — The funder side: sign up and pick your lane

**Screen:** Click "I'm a Funder" → lands on `/get-started/funder`, the six-card type chooser.

**Voiceover:**
"Founda21 isn't one-size-fits-all. Whether you're a corporate ESD programme, an ESD fund manager, a DFI, a university, an accelerator, or an impact investor — you tell us who you are once, and the entire dashboard and every report reframes around what matters to you."

**Action:** Click "DFI / government programme" → lands on `/signup?type=dfi_government` with that option pre-selected.

**Voiceover (continued):**
"Sign up in under a minute. No procurement process, no integration project."

**Action:** Fill organisation name, email, password → submit → lands on `/dashboard`.

---

## 1:45–2:30 — Issue a passcode, not a link to chase

**Screen:** `/dashboard/cohorts/new` → fill cohort name, set intended entry stage, max uses, expiry → submit → lands on cohort detail page.

**Voiceover:**
"Create a cohort — for this quarter's intake, this pilot, this fund. Set how many founders can use it and when it expires. You get a passcode: a real control point, not an open link anyone can forward indefinitely."

**Visual beat:** Zoom on the large monospace passcode ("ZEK8FFSX") and the "Uses: 1/1" line.

**Voiceover (continued):**
"No founder pays for this. Ever. Founda21's founder experience has zero pricing, zero checkout, zero upsell — access is entirely through the passcode you control."

---

## 2:30–3:15 — The founder side, compressed

**Screen:** New tab, `/get-started/founder`. Show the passcode field, then a fast montage (sped-up, 2–3x) scrolling through the intake form: full name, venture, venture stage, ownership/entity fields, outcome snapshot, email/password.

**Voiceover:**
"A founder with your passcode signs up once. Alongside the standard venture details, we capture B-BBEE ownership, entity type, and a baseline outcome snapshot — headcount, revenue, capital raised. This data feeds your eligibility and impact reporting later. It never touches the AI scoring engine. Scoring is judged on the founder's work — not on who they are."

**Action:** Submit → lands on `/founder`, checkpoint list for Stage 1.

**Voiceover (continued):**
"From here, the founder works through real checkpoints — problem definition, market sizing, unit economics, cap table hygiene, pitch readiness — each one scored by AI against a fixed threshold. Stage 2 doesn't unlock until Stage 1 is genuinely passed. There's no skipping ahead."

---

## 3:15–4:00 — The dual output: what you actually get back

**Screen:** Cut to a founder who has already passed Stage 1 (use a seeded/pre-scored account for this beat). Show `/dashboard/founders/[id]` overview — checkpoint grid lighting up green, "Founda21 Investable" badge state.

**Voiceover:**
"When a founder clears a stage, you get two things — not one. The founder gets a portable, verifiable credential they carry with them, even to a different funder. And you get an Institutional Report."

**Action:** Click "Institutional reports →" → lands on `/dashboard/founders/[id]/reports`.

**Voiceover (continued):**
"This is framed for exactly what you told us you are. A DFI sees outcomes first — capital raised, jobs, survival. A corporate ESD programme sees B-BBEE beneficiary eligibility first. A university sees development delta — how far this founder moved since the last checkpoint. Same underlying data, reordered around what your board actually asks you to report."

**Visual beat:** Show the eligibility badges (ESD beneficiary eligible, EME/QSE, Black women-owned) and the development-gap list with score deltas.

---

## 4:00–4:35 — Zoom out: the whole cohort

**Screen:** Back to cohort detail page. Show the M&E summary tiles (ESD eligible, Black women-owned, total capital raised, still operating), then click "Export CSV" and show the file briefly (or a rendered table).

**Voiceover:**
"Zoom out to the whole cohort and it's the same story at scale — pass rates per stage, beneficiary class breakdown, aggregate outcomes, all exportable into a single file. This is built to drop straight into a B-BBEE verification pack or a DFI progress report, not to be rebuilt from scratch every quarter."

---

## 4:35–5:00 — Close

**Screen:** Back to the landing page, hold on the two CTAs.

**Voiceover:**
"Founda21 doesn't ask you to trust a founder's pitch. It gives you a standard, evidence at every checkpoint, and a report that speaks your language — whether that's B-BBEE, jobs, or growth. One standard. Every funder type. Founders who actually clear it."

**On-screen text (final card):**
"Founda21 — The Founder Readiness Standard.
Get started at [your domain] / [get-started/funder]"

**End.**
