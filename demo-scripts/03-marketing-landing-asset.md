# Founda21 Demo Script — Marketing / Landing Page Asset

**Length:** 0:75–1:15 (75 seconds target). A 5-minute cut does not work as a marketing asset — attention drops off hard after ~90 seconds for this kind of clip, so this one is intentionally short. If you want a longer version for a specific context (e.g. an embedded product page), extend using the "optional extended cut" note at the end rather than padding this version.

**Audience:** Cold traffic landing on the website / social. They don't know what Founda21 is yet.
**Tone:** Fast, confident, high energy. Quick cuts, not lingering shots. Think product-launch trailer, not tutorial.
**Music:** Upbeat, drives the whole thing, no silence.
**Voice:** Punchy short lines. No sentence over ~12 words on screen at once.

---

## 0:00–0:08 — Hook

**Screen:** Bold text on dark background, fast cut.

**On-screen text (flash, ~1.5s each):**
- "Pitch decks lie."
- "Self-assessments lie."
- "Founda21 doesn't."

**Voiceover (optional, matches text):**
"Pitch decks lie. Self-assessments lie. Founda21 doesn't."

---

## 0:08–0:20 — What it is

**Screen:** Cut to landing page hero, fast zoom on headline "Know which founders are actually investable."

**Voiceover:**
"21 checkpoints. Five dimensions. Scored by AI. One standard — every founder, every funder."

**On-screen text overlay:** "21 CHECKPOINTS · 3 STAGES · AI-SCORED"

---

## 0:20–0:35 — Funder side, fast

**Screen:** Quick-cut montage (0.5–1s per frame): funder type chooser → signup form filling itself in (sped up) → dashboard → cohort with passcode glowing.

**Voiceover:**
"Sign up as any funder type. Issue a passcode. That's it."

**On-screen text overlay (appears over passcode shot):** "NO LINKS TO CHASE. JUST A CODE."

---

## 0:35–0:50 — Founder side, fast

**Screen:** Quick-cut: founder enters passcode → checkpoint grid → a checkpoint turning from grey to green (score reveal, punchy sound effect).

**Voiceover:**
"Founders never pay. They prove it — checkpoint by checkpoint."

**On-screen text overlay:** "FOUNDERS NEVER PAY. EVER."

---

## 0:50–1:05 — The payoff

**Screen:** Split-screen or quick cut between the founder's "Founda21 Investable" badge and the funder's institutional report screen (eligibility badges, development delta).

**Voiceover:**
"One credential. Two views. Everything your report needs — already built."

**On-screen text overlay:** "PORTABLE CREDENTIAL + FUNDER-READY REPORTS"

---

## 1:05–1:15 — Close / CTA

**Screen:** Back to landing page, hold on the two CTA cards ("I'm a Funder" / "I'm a Founder").

**Voiceover:**
"Founda21. The Founder Readiness Standard."

**On-screen text (final card, holds 2s):**
"FOUNDA21.
Know who's actually ready.
[your domain]"

**End.**
